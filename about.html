Her Story

Where Psychology, Education & Human Development Meet.

For more than two decades, Dr. Faten Skafi's professional journey has been shaped by one central purpose: helping people learn, grow and experience greater psychological well-being.

As an Assistant Professor and Educational Psychologist, her work connects clinical psychology, educational psychology, mental health, research, student development and professional training.

Her experience extends across universities, psychological and psychoeducational practice, school leadership, curriculum development, research supervision, community mental health and work with children, adolescents and families.

Understanding the person is the first step toward meaningful learning, healthier development and lasting change.
